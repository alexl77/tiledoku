Tiledoku

Picture sudoku with cute tiles

Contact

Questions, bug reports and ideas for new tile sets are all welcome. Email tiledoku.app@outlook.com and I'll get back to you.

If something went wrong, it helps to mention your device, the iOS version and what you were doing.

How do I play?

Fill the grid so every row, column and 3×3 box holds each of the nine tiles exactly once. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

How do I restore my purchases?

Tap any locked tile set, then tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought them with.

What do the packs include?

Each pack unlocks a group of themed tile sets, and any set added to a pack later is yours too. Tiledoku Plus unlocks every set, including all future ones. All purchases are one-time, with no subscription.

When does the daily puzzle change?

At midnight on your device. It's the same puzzle for everyone that day and free for all players.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Deleting the app deletes your stats and streak; purchases can always be restored.
