Tiledoku Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

The short version

Tiledoku doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account to create.

What stays on your device

Your games in progress, settings, stats and daily streak are saved only on your device. They're never sent to me or anyone else, and deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your name, email or payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, Tiledoku sends your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. This is optional, managed by Apple and controlled in your device's Settings.

Children

Tiledoku is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email tiledoku.app@outlook.com.
